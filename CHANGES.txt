DEAD SIGNAL - Changes from the One-Pager
Zachary Stroozas - CS583 Individual 2D Project

Art and sound: all sprites were drawn procedurally in code and all sound effects were synthesized
(no downloaded assets), so the look and sound are original to this project.

KEPT FROM THE DESIGN
- Player explores a dark, damaged space station with a glowing signal orb.
- WASD/arrows to move, Space to pulse, Escape to pause.
- Pulse reveals nearby walls, equipment, survivors and enemies, but also alerts enemies.
- 5 survivors, 2 enemy types, 1 escape pod, 1 level, energy meter, survivor counter, objective text, win/lose screens.
- Sound effects only, no background music.
- Win: rescue all 5 survivors and reach the pod. Lose: energy hits 0 or too much damage.

CHANGES / DETAILS ADDED DURING DEVELOPMENT
1. Pulse "hearing" vs "seeing": a pulse reveals 7 tiles around you, but enemies hear it up to 12 tiles away.
   This makes the core twist sharper, because the signal always gives away more than it shows you.
2. Reveal wave: objects light up as the expanding ring reaches them instead of all at once.
3. Station memory: walls and survivors you have already revealed stay faintly visible. Enemies do NOT stay visible.
   This was added to solve the "make the dark readable" challenge from the one-pager.
4. Small passive glow around the orb so the player is never 100% blind right next to walls.
5. Energy drains slowly over time as well as per pulse, so the energy lose condition actually matters
   even for a player who never pulses.
6. Health = 3 hits, with a short invulnerability blink and screen shake after each hit. After landing a hit,
   an enemy freezes for 1.5 seconds and lights up so the player can see what hit them and escape (added after
   playtesting showed unseen enemies could chain-hit a player who stood still).
7. Enemy types got specific behaviors:
   - Hunter (red): dormant and blind. Sprints to the location of any pulse it hears, searches, then goes dormant again.
   - Drifter (purple): wanders on its own, chases if it sees you (walls block its sight), and investigates pulses.
   Enemies use grid pathfinding (breadth-first search) so they route around walls.
8. "Enemy movement/warning sound" became a proximity alarm that beeps faster as the nearest enemy gets closer.
   This gives the player an audio-only way to sense danger without pulsing.
9. Doors from the art list became open doorways (no door mechanic) to keep scope manageable.
10. Escape pod is locked (with a message and buzz) until all survivors are rescued (see #20 for the drop-off update). It then powers on, stays
    visible, and plays an activation + launch sequence before the win screen.
11. The level is built from a text file (Assets/Levels/Level1.txt) so the layout is easy to edit and tune.

ROUND 2 - ADDED AFTER PLAYTESTING (the game felt too easy)
12. The player is now an astronaut (a crewmate of the 5 missing survivors) carrying the signal orb,
    instead of a floating orb.
13. Rescued survivors follow the player in a line (each one walks along the player's breadcrumb trail).
14. Enemies can snatch survivors out of the line. A snatched survivor runs back to where it was found
    and has to be rescued again, so a long line is a liability you have to protect.
15. Each survivor in the line slows the player by 5%. With all 5, the player is slower than a Hunter.
16. Powering on the escape pod sets off a station alarm. Every Hunter rushes to the pod and patrols around it,
    turning the ending into a final sneak/sprint.
17. Restless Hunters: after the 4th pulse, Hunters no longer go back to sleep. They prowl the station instead,
    which punishes spamming the pulse.
18. Rebalance: pulse cost 7 -> 10 energy, passive drain 0.6 -> 0.7 per second, each rescue gives back
    20 energy, and a third Drifter was added (2 Hunters, 3 Drifters).
19. New sounds: station alarm and survivor-lost wail.

ROUND 3 - ADDED AFTER PLAYTESTING (a 5-survivor line was too hard to protect)
20. The escape pod is now also a safe drop-off point. Touching it with survivors following drops them all
    off, and survivors inside the pod are safe for good (they can't be snatched again). The player can bring
    survivors back in small groups. The alarm now goes off when the last survivor is found, so the final
    trip back to the pod is still a race past the Hunters. The pod launches when all 5 are safe inside.
21. HUD now shows "Safe in pod" and "Following you" counts, and the red bar is labeled HEALTH.

ROUND 4 - POLISH AND SURVIVABILITY
22. Health is now 100 points instead of 3 hits. Enemies deal 34 damage, so it is still 3 hits from full
    (difficulty unchanged). The HUD shows the exact number next to the health bar.
23. Health packs: 4 medkits spawn on random floor tiles at the start (never right next to the player), and
    another spawns every 40 seconds (max 4 on the map). Each heals 15 health (15% of max). They are hidden in
    the dark like everything else, so finding them means risking a pulse. They aren't used up at full health.
24. Title screen with the game name, a one-line story, controls, and Start/Quit buttons. Restarting after a
    win or loss skips it.
25. Run timer at the top of the screen. The win screen shows your time and your best time (saved between runs).
26. Low-energy signal hum: a looping static/electrical hum that is silent above 50% energy, then gets louder,
    lower-pitched, and more distorted as the signal dies. This is gameplay-tied audio (it tells you how close
    you are to losing), not background music.
27. New sounds: heal chime and signal hum loop.

ROUND 5 - DIFFICULTY PASS (full runs were too hard to beat)
28. Enemies deal 25 damage instead of 34 (4 hits from full health instead of 3).
29. After landing a hit, enemies freeze for 2.5s instead of 1.5s, and the player is invulnerable for 2s
    instead of 1.5s, so there is a real window to escape instead of getting chain-hit.
30. Energy: pulse cost 10 -> 7, passive drain 0.7 -> 0.5 per second.
31. Hunters become restless after 6 pulses instead of 4.
32. Hunter speed 3.4 -> 3.1 (still close to a player leading a full line). Drifter chase speed 2.8 -> 2.5,
    Drifter sight range 4.5 -> 4 tiles.

ROUND 6 - TIME LIMIT
33. The run timer is now a 3:00 countdown. The timer turns red with a warning alarm at 30 seconds left, and
    the player loses if it reaches 0:00. The win screen still shows how long the run took and the best time.
